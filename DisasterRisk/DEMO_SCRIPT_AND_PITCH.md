# ClaimPulse — 90-Second Demo Script & Pitch Outline

## 90-Second Live Demo Script

### 0:00–0:15 — The Problem
> "When a major flood hits coastal Odisha, satellites capture the water footprint within hours—but insurers, governments, and banks still spend weeks turning pixels into financial decisions. People wait for emergency relief while claims sit in manual queues. **ClaimPulse** turns any satellite disaster observation into an explainable economic decision in seconds."

### 0:15–0:35 — Satellite Detection & Spatial Exposure
> "Here in our Mission Control console, I select the **Coastal Flood — Odisha, India** event. Instantly, ClaimPulse overlays **Sentinel-1 SAR flood extent** and **Copernicus DEM depth** with **OpenStreetMap buildings, roads, critical infrastructure, FAO cropland, WorldPop population**, and a synthetic **insurance portfolio**. I can toggle the **Before / After Satellite Slider** to inspect pre-event vs. post-event detection, or click any zone or hospital on the map to inspect the exact evidence."

### 0:35–0:55 — Transparent Economic Loss Calculation
> "In the right panel, ClaimPulse converts hazard intensity into Indian Rupees: **₹1,173.76 Cr total expected loss** across **566 km²**, **24,000+ buildings**, and **1,45,000+ residents**, broken down by Housing, Agriculture, Infrastructure, and Business Interruption—plus the **Top 10 Highest-Loss Relief Zones**. And every number is transparent: we expose the exact formula—*Hazard Intensity × Exposure × Vulnerability × Damage Multiplier*—along with uncertainty bounds and dataset timestamps."

### 0:55–1:15 — Explainable Decisions & Grounded AI Analyst
> "Next, ClaimPulse outputs actionable decisions: **4 parametric claims** qualify for **Auto-fast-track liquidity**, while high-value or lower-confidence claims route to **Mandatory Human Review**—we never auto-deny a claim. Watch as I approve a claim: it records an immutable audit entry and fires a partner webhook. And in **Ask ClaimPulse**, I can ask *'How would a 20% increase in flood depth change the payout?'*—ClaimPulse re-runs the live model and cites exact layers and timestamps."

### 1:15–1:30 — Modular Platform & Export
> "Finally, any partner team can plug into ClaimPulse's **Connected Modules** registry or download a **one-page PDF Decision Brief, CSV ledger, or GeoJSON** in one click. ClaimPulse isn't just a damage map—it's the decision-support engine for disaster finance."

## Pitch Slide Outline (8 Slides)

1. **Title:** *ClaimPulse — Turning Satellite Pixels into Disaster Finance Decisions.*
2. **The Bottleneck:** Satellites see disasters in hours; insurance payouts, relief budgets, and loan risk updates take weeks.
3. **The Solution:** End-to-end automated pipeline: Satellite Hazard → Asset Exposure → Transparent Economic Loss → Stakeholder Decisions.
4. **Live Product Architecture:** Flood (default) + Wildfire + Cyclone modules powered by Sentinel-1/2, NASA VIIRS/FIRMS, Copernicus DEM, OSM, and WorldPop.
5. **Explainable Economics & Governance:** Deterministic loss formula, `-18% / +21%` uncertainty bounds, zero auto-denials, human-in-the-loop audit trail.
6. **Multi-Stakeholder Value:** Insurers (fast-track triage), Governments (top-10 relief allocation), Lenders (collateral LTV stress & bps pricing), Businesses & NGOs.
7. **Grounded AI Analyst & Open Plugin Ecosystem:** Live counterfactual stress testing + REST/Webhook integration contract for partner teams.
8. **Roadmap & Ask:** Live GEE/STAC streaming, Overture building footprints, and parametric disbursement rails.
